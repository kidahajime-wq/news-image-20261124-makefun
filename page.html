KOREA · EAST SEA

JETS SCRAMBLE AS RUSSIAN WARPLANES BUZZ ISLAND

SEOUL — About five Russian warplanes entered South Korea’s air defence zone early on Tuesday, flying toward Ulleung Island and forcing the Air Force to scramble fighter jets.

The military issued a rare air-raid warning telling the island’s 9,000 residents to take shelter — then lifted it three minutes later. No airspace was violated, Seoul said.
